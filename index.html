Academic Excellence & Diplomatic Expertise

With over 15 years of experience in academia and international relations, Dr. Meraj Ahmad has established himself as a prominent figure in diplomatic studies and educational leadership. His research focuses on international security, conflict resolution, and diplomatic history.

Dr. Ahmad has served as a visiting professor at several prestigious institutions worldwide and has contributed to numerous international conferences and diplomatic initiatives. His work bridges the gap between academic theory and practical diplomacy.

Academic Contributions

Selected Publications

A sample of scholarly works contributing to the field of international relations.

"Diplomatic Recognition in the 21st Century: Evolving Norms and Practices"

2021 International Studies Quarterly

This article examines how the practice of diplomatic recognition has evolved in response to changing global power dynamics and the emergence of new state and non-state actors in international relations.

"South Asian Security Dilemmas: Nuclear Proliferation and Regional Stability"

2019 Journal of Strategic Studies

An analysis of the security challenges facing South Asia in the context of nuclear proliferation, with particular focus on India-Pakistan relations and the role of major powers in the region.

"The Diplomacy of Small States in a Multipolar World"

2017 Cambridge University Press

This book explores how small states navigate the complexities of contemporary international relations, with case studies from Southeast Asia, the Caribbean, and Africa.

"Track II Diplomacy in Conflict Resolution: Lessons from South Asia"

2015 Conflict Resolution Quarterly

An examination of unofficial diplomatic channels in South Asian conflict resolution, analyzing their effectiveness and limitations in comparison to formal diplomatic processes.
